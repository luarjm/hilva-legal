Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Hilva is a paid app with a single, one-off payment. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no ads: every feature is included.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

For the AI features (spotting tasks in what you say, summaries, suggesting where a note goes) you need a device with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it Hilva still records, transcribes and organises your voice with fixed rules (basic mode).

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no sign-up. Hilva works offline; iCloud sync uses the iCloud account already on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I split what I say into lines?

Just talk: a pause starts a new line, and sentences with several things ("tomorrow buy cheese and on Friday call the plumber") become several lines with their dates. In Spanish and English you can also say "inside", "outside", "new point" or "task".

The first recording takes a while or says the voice model is not ready.

The first time, iOS downloads the speech model for your language. Keep the device connected to the internet and try again in a few minutes (Settings › Models in Hilva shows the progress).

The Apple Watch recording did not reach my iPhone.

Open Hilva on your iPhone: recordings from the watch arrive in the Inbox at the top of Home. Both devices must be paired and nearby.

How does the spoken review work?

When you have tasks for today or overdue, a card appears on Home. Tap "Review aloud": Hilva reads each task and you answer "done", "tomorrow", "Friday", "next", "delete" or "stop". You can also use the buttons, and undo the whole review at the end.
