Hilva Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Summary: Hilva does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. Your notes and voice recordings stay on your own devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud, which only you can access. Transcription and artificial intelligence run on your device.

1. What data we collect

None. Hilva does not request, collect or transmit any personally identifiable information: no name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, device identifiers or usage data. The developer cannot see your notes or recordings.

2. What the app stores and where

Hilva stores the following only on your device, in a storage area shared between the app and its widgets:

  • Your notes and outlines: the text of each line, its order, tasks, dates and whether they are done.
  • Your voice recordings (audio files) and their transcriptions, so you can listen to each line as you said it.
  • Summaries and suggestions made on your device by Apple Intelligence.
  • Your settings (appearance, reminders, iCloud sync on or off).

This information is not sent to any server of ours and is not shared with third parties. Uninstalling the app deletes it from that device.

3. Microphone, transcription and artificial intelligence

Hilva uses the microphone only while you are recording (and, during the spoken review, for a few seconds after reading each task, to hear your answer; that audio is not kept). Speech is transcribed on your device with Apple's speech framework. The artificial intelligence features (organising lines, spotting tasks, summaries) use Apple's on-device model through Apple Intelligence. Hilva does not send your audio or your text to any server for transcription or AI.

Apple may download speech or language models to your device the first time they are needed; this is handled by iOS. See Apple's own privacy information for Apple Intelligence and speech recognition.

4. iCloud sync

If iCloud sync is on (you can turn it off in Hilva's Settings), your notes and recordings are also kept in the private database of your own iCloud account, so your iPhone, iPad and other devices signed in to it stay up to date. This uses Apple's CloudKit: the data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account, only your own devices can access it and the developer cannot see it.

5. Apple Watch

If you record on Apple Watch, the audio is sent directly to your paired iPhone, which transcribes it. This uses Apple's device-to-device connection between your own watch and iPhone; no server of ours is involved.

6. Calendar, reminders and search

"Add to Calendar" opens Apple's own event editor with the line already filled in. The first time, Hilva asks for permission to access your calendars, only so it can remember which event it created and open that same event again (to see or change it) instead of adding a duplicate. Hilva only looks up the events it created itself, and nothing leaves your device. If you don't allow access, the event editor still works and Hilva only remembers that the line was added. Task reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Your notes can appear in Spotlight search on your device; this index stays on the device.

7. Internet connection

Hilva works offline. It only uses the network for iCloud sync and for the speech and language models that iOS may download, both handled by the system.

8. Third parties

Hilva does not include any advertising, analytics, social network or tracking SDK. No third party has access to your information.

9. Children

Hilva is not directed at children under 13 and, as it collects no data, it cannot collect information from minors.

10. Payments

Hilva is a paid app with a single purchase made through the App Store. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the developer never receives your payment details.

11. Your rights

Since no data is collected, there is nothing to request, rectify or delete on our side. You can delete any note or recording in the app, delete Hilva's data from your iCloud in iOS Settings (Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage) and uninstall the app from your devices.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
